# Influences (honest)

## Dark City (1998)
Strong thematic kinship: city as rewritten prison, memory politics, identity under experiment, enforcers of the lie, escape through understanding the system.

Cold Boot diverges by making the player an **editor of causality** (SCAN/SNAP/SUNDER), dual explicit timelines (1994 Necropolis / 2026 Vesper), and adaptive constraint adversaries (Auditors) instead of only conspirators in trench coats.

## Noctropolis (1994)
Spiritual successor energy: comic/gothic immersion, identity shift, not a trademark/asset clone.

## Design rule
Homage through structure and mood. Original names, systems, and art only.
